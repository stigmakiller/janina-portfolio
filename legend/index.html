I · The Legend

Touch a button,
catch the luck

Across European folklore the chimney sweep is a bringer of good fortune. Meet one on the street, touch a button of his coat, and luck is said to follow you home. At New Year he appears on cards and charms beside clovers and horseshoes.

Our bottle carries him in silver relief — top hat, ladder and a cat at his heels — above a city of copper roofs. A toast to the small rituals people keep for luck.
